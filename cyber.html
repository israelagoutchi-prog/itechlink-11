What We Deliver

Practical protection across your whole business.

Security works best in layers. We assess where you are exposed, then put sensible controls in place across your systems, people and data.

Security Assessment

We review your current setup to identify weaknesses and the risks that matter most to your business.

  • Vulnerability checks
  • Risk review
  • Clear recommendations

Network Security

Protecting the connections your business depends on, from the office network to remote access.

  • Firewall configuration
  • Secure Wi-Fi & VPN
  • Network segmentation

Endpoint Protection

Keeping laptops, desktops and mobile devices protected against malware and other threats.

  • Antivirus & anti-malware
  • Device policies
  • Patch and update management

Email & Phishing Protection

Reducing the risk from scam emails and fraudulent messages, a common way attackers get in.

  • Spam & phishing filtering
  • Email authentication
  • Safer email settings

Data Protection & Backup

Helping make sure important business data is protected, recoverable and only accessible to the right people.

  • Backup planning
  • Access control
  • Recovery testing

Security Awareness Training

Your staff are part of your defence. We help them spot threats and follow safe working habits.

  • Phishing awareness
  • Password & MFA guidance
  • Security policies
Our Process

From unknown risks to a stronger security position.

We follow a clear, step-by-step approach so you always know what we found, what we are changing and why.

01

Assess

We review your systems, devices, access and data to find weaknesses and priorities.

02

Plan

We agree a practical security roadmap that fits your business, risks and budget.

03

Protect

We put the agreed controls in place across networks, endpoints, email and data.

04

Train

We help your team understand common threats and how to respond safely.

05

Review & Improve

We revisit your security regularly as your business and the threats change.

Layered Security

No single tool is enough on its own.

Combining several layers of protection means that if one control fails, others are still there to reduce the impact.

01

Network

Firewalls and secure connections that control what enters and leaves.

02

Devices

Protection and updates on the laptops, desktops and phones your team uses.

03

Identity & Access

Strong passwords, multi-factor authentication and sensible permissions.

04

Data

Backups and controls that protect information and help you recover.
